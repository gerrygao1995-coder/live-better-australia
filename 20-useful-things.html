Australian life.
A few things worth knowing.

Twenty useful starting points from the full guide. Choose what fits, check the source, and pass on the one a friend could use.

An editorial selection, not a ranking of guaranteed savings or outcomes. Eligibility and state rules matter.

01 / Everyday costs

Find the energy comparison service for your state

Use the right official tool and your actual usage; retailer choice and comparison coverage vary across Australia.

Compare whole-bill costs using actual usage, tariffs and supply charges. Energy Made Easy covers NSW, Queensland, SA, Tasmania and the ACT; retail choice varies locally. Victoria uses Victorian Energy Compare. For WA and the NT, follow the national guide, and check solar, demand charges and contract conditions.

Basis: Official / professional guidance · Reviewed 2026-10-09

Applies to: Energy customers; embedded networks and some regional areas have different options.

02 / Healthcare and medicines

The Medicare registration couples can miss

Sharing a Medicare card does not automatically combine your family's eligible Safety Net costs.

Check whether you should register as a couple or family so eligible medical costs can be combined. Sharing a Medicare card does not register a Safety Net family; keep details current and respond to requests to confirm family members.

Applies to: Medicare families and couples; definitions and thresholds apply.

03 / Consumer and legal rights

Your rights can outlast the product warranty

Australian consumer guarantees may still apply after a warranty expires; the fault determines the available remedy.

Keep purchase evidence and ask the seller for the remedy matching the fault. Major product problems generally allow a refund-or-replacement choice; minor problems usually allow the business to fix them. Warranty expiry does not automatically end consumer guarantees; change-of-mind returns and many private sales have different rules.

Basis: Official rule · Reviewed 2026-10-09

Applies to: Covered consumer purchases; product, seller and type of failure affect rights.

04 / Super, tax and insurance

Check your cover before merging super accounts

Closing a super account can end insurance or benefits you may struggle to replace.

Compare fees, insurance and benefits before consolidating super. Closing an account can end valuable cover; confirm any replacement protection first and check employer benefits and tax consequences. Seek professional advice before transferring a defined benefit account, where leaving may surrender benefits you cannot regain.

Applies to: People with multiple super accounts or considering a fund change.

05 / Renting and buying a home

A bond dispute can have a short reply window

Read a bond claim promptly and follow your own state's process before agreeing to deductions.

Check your local bond authority's refund and dispute procedure, read notices promptly and retain evidence. NSW disputes use the prescribed Fair Trading and NCAT process, with short response periods. Follow your own jurisdiction's instructions before agreeing to deductions or assuming a claim will resolve itself.

Basis: Official rule · Reviewed 2026-10-09

Applies to: NSW example; other jurisdictions have their own bond authority and procedure.

06 / Work rights

Check your right to disconnect after work

National-system employees can refuse after-hours contact unless unreasonable; the job and circumstances matter.

Agree normal availability, emergency contact and on-call arrangements in writing. National-system employees, including small-business employees since August 2025, can refuse out-of-hours work contact unless refusal is unreasonable. The reason, disruption, compensation, role and personal circumstances matter; the rule does not prohibit employers sending messages.

Basis: Official rule · Reviewed 2026-10-09

Applies to: Employees covered by the national right to disconnect; other arrangements may provide additional rights.

07 / Super, tax and insurance

Ambulance cover changes across state borders

Medicare does not cover ambulance services; residency, interstate travel and policy terms can affect your cover.

Check state ambulance arrangements and your actual cover; Medicare does not cover ambulance services. Queensland and Tasmania have state-funded resident arrangements, while other jurisdictions differ. Check interstate travel, non-emergency services, waiting periods and exclusions. In a life-threatening emergency, call 000 and seek help promptly.

Basis: Official rule · Reviewed 2026-10-09

Applies to: Australian residents, particularly after moving interstate or changing insurance.

08 / Help when money is tight and disability support

Start with free help for debt

The National Debt Helpline connects people in financial difficulty with free, confidential financial counselling.

Contact the National Debt Helpline on 1800 007 007 for free, confidential financial counselling. Bring available income, expense and debt information plus urgent notices. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples can also use Mob Strong Debt Help on 1800 808 488; check current service availability.

09 / Work rights

Check whether that trial shift should be paid

Fair Work limits unpaid trials to a necessary, supervised skills demonstration; an ordinary shift is not automatically free.

Ask what skill you will demonstrate, who will supervise and how long it will take. Fair Work says an unpaid trial must be limited to what is reasonably needed to demonstrate relevant skills under direct supervision; productive work beyond that can require payment. There is no universal free-shift allowance.

Basis: Official rule · Reviewed 2026-10-09

Applies to: Applicants in the national workplace system.

10 / Relationships and children

Compare childcare fees after the subsidy

The 3 Day Guarantee is subsidised care for eligible families; gap fees and available places still matter.

Check CCS eligibility and ask providers for estimates using your attendance and subsidy. Since January 2026, the 3 Day Guarantee offers eligible families at least three days of subsidised care weekly, with a gap fee still payable. It does not guarantee a place; compare sessions, extras, travel and the current subsidy calculation.

Basis: Official rule · Reviewed 2026-10-09

Applies to: Families meeting CCS conditions; service availability and charges vary locally.

11 / Relationships and children

Plan parental pay around your child's arrival date

The birth or adoption date affects available Parental Leave Pay days; eligibility and sharing rules also apply.

Check eligibility and claiming rules before planning leave around government payments. For births or adoptions from July 2026, eligible families can receive up to 130 Parental Leave Pay days, subject to work, income, residence and caring rules. Sharing and reserved days apply; separately confirm employer pay and the right to time off.

Basis: Official rule · Reviewed 2026-10-09

Applies to: Eligible parents and carers; the child's birth or adoption date and family circumstances matter.

12 / Education and career

Check your state's Free TAFE options

Selected courses and students can receive tuition support, while materials, placements and other costs still need checking.

Follow government Free TAFE information to your state or territory's current programme. Funding covers selected courses and eligibility groups, not every course or applicant. Confirm your place, tuition assistance, materials, placement costs and conditions in writing before comparing the full cost with other training options.

13 / Transport and car ownership

Search the PPSR before buying a used car

An official VIN search can reveal registered finance and available vehicle-history records before you pay.

Check the vehicle identification number against the actual vehicle and use the official PPSR search before a used-car purchase. Read the result and retain the certificate; a search can reveal registered security interests and available vehicle-history information but does not establish mechanical condition.

Basis: Official / professional guidance · Reviewed 2026-10-09

Applies to: Used-car buyers; inspect the certificate and its limitations.

14 / Transport and car ownership

CTP and car-damage cover do different jobs

Compulsory third-party cover should not be assumed to pay for damage to your car or someone else's.

Check exactly what compulsory third-party insurance and your other motor policy cover. Compare third-party property and comprehensive options with excesses and exclusions; compulsory cover should not be assumed to pay for damage you cause to someone else's car or your own.

15 / Help when money is tight and disability support

NDIS and DSP need separate eligibility checks

Disability supports and income support have different rules; approval for one does not establish eligibility for the other.

Check NDIS supports and Disability Support Pension separately: each has its own eligibility and evidence requirements. Eligibility for one does not establish the other. Follow the relevant agency's guidance, retain decisions and ask about reviews. An NDIS partner may also identify community and mainstream support.

Basis: Official rule · Reviewed 2026-10-09

Applies to: People with disability and their supporters; diagnosis alone does not establish entitlement.

16 / Healthcare and medicines

Ask whether a 60-day prescription suits you

For eligible medicines and suitable patients, a longer supply may mean fewer pharmacy visits and lower dispensing costs.

Ask your prescriber whether an eligible medicine and stable ongoing condition make a 60-day prescription suitable. A new prescription and clinical assessment are required; a larger supply changes collection frequency, not the dose you should take.

Basis: Official rule · Reviewed 2026-10-09

Applies to: Eligible medicines and suitable patients; not a universal entitlement.

Check travel assistance before a medical trip

State patient travel schemes may help eligible regional patients; check approval and evidence rules before booking.

18 / Climate, disasters and resilient homes

Check flood and bushfire risk for the exact address

Local hazard information belongs alongside inspections and insurance quotes when choosing where to live.

Use council and state information to investigate flood, bushfire and other hazards before renting, buying or planning works. Ask what the maps do and do not show, and combine that information with inspection and insurance enquiries rather than treating any one map as a guarantee.

19 / Everyday health

Check UV even when the weather feels cool

Australia's UV forecast can call for sun protection on a cool or cloudy day.

Check the UV index and combine clothing, shade, a protective hat, sunglasses and sunscreen when UV reaches 3 or higher. Choose broad-spectrum, water-resistant SPF50 or SPF50+ sunscreen and follow its directions; cool or cloudy weather does not guarantee low UV.

Basis: Official / professional guidance · Reviewed 2026-10-09

Applies to: People outdoors; follow individual advice for photosensitivity.

20 / Healthcare and medicines

Find your local Medicare Urgent Care Clinic

Check a local option for urgent, non-life-threatening care; life-threatening emergencies still need 000 or an emergency department.

Locate your nearest Medicare Urgent Care Clinic and check its hours. These clinics handle urgent problems that are not life-threatening when timely GP care is unavailable; for a life-threatening illness or injury, call 000 or attend an emergency department instead.

Basis: Official rule · Reviewed 2026-10-09

Applies to: Check local services and Medicare arrangements before relying on access.
