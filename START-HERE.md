# One useful action is enough to start

A guide this large should make life easier to navigate. Use the parts that fit your life; you do not need to turn it into a scorecard.

## If you have ten minutes

1. Pick your current pressure point in [Find your situation](SCENARIOS.md).
2. Open its first chapter and choose one action you can realistically take.
3. Check **Applies to** and the source before relying on a rule. Save the action ID if you want to return to it.
4. Write down the next call, appointment, comparison or document you need. Stop there if that is enough for today.

## Keep a compact life-admin record

Use a private place you already trust. Record what each document is, who provides it, when it needs attention and how an authorised person can find it. Categories might include housing, health, work, insurance, accounts, dependants and an emergency contact. Do not put passwords, identity documents or personal case histories in this public repository.

## A practical decision filter

Before spending time or money, ask:

- Is this the problem I actually need to solve?
- What happens if I wait, and is there a real deadline?
- What is the full cost, including upkeep, travel and my time?
- Does this rule or service apply to my state, circumstances and date?
- What would a small, reversible first step look like?
- Who can help me verify the part I do not understand?

The evidence labels describe the basis of an entry, not a ranking of what matters most in your life. [Read the editorial policy](EDITORIAL-POLICY.md).
