## AU-0253 · Protect your main email with MFA

Enable multi-factor authentication on your primary email and financial accounts. Follow each provider’s setup instructions and store recovery codes securely away from the device you depend on.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Usually no fee; setup time. |
| Potential value | An extra barrier to account takeover. |
| Applies to | Anyone with online accounts. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Australian Signals Directorate — Personal cyber security first steps](https://www.cyber.gov.au/sites/default/files/2024-05/personal-cyber-security-first-steps-guide.pdf)

## AU-0254 · Give every important account its own password

Use a reputable password manager or unique long passphrases. Secure the manager itself, keep recovery instructions safe and check bank terms before storing banking credentials in it.

## AU-0258 · Set up a lost-phone plan before losing it

## AU-0260 · Keep recovery contacts current

Review the recovery email and phone number on essential accounts after changing jobs or telcos. Remove obsolete methods only after a new recovery method works. Do not make a former employer’s address your sole recovery route.

## AU-0261 · Verify changed bank details outside the message

If an invoice suddenly gives new bank details, contact the supplier through a number you already trust. Treat the requested change as unverified until confirmed. An email thread or familiar logo alone is not enough.

## AU-0268 · Read a breach notice for the action required

Go to the affected provider’s official channel, confirm the notice and follow its instructions. Change exposed credentials and get identity support if needed. Avoid a stranger offering paid recovery through the notice itself.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Time; support is often available. |
| Potential value | A response matched to what was exposed. |
| Applies to | People notified of an account or data breach. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |
