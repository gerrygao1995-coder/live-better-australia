## AU-0127 · Check that super reaches the fund

Compare contributions received by your super fund with payslips and employment arrangements. Check personal details and the contribution destination; a payslip figure is not proof the fund received money. Query discrepancies with the fund and payroll, then use the ATO's current unpaid-super process if unresolved.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A brief check and follow-up where needed. |
| Potential value | Helps detect missing contributions and administrative errors earlier. |
| Applies to | Workers entitled to employer super; special eligibility rules can apply. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Moneysmart: How to check your super](https://moneysmart.gov.au/grow-your-super/how-to-check-your-super) · [ATO: Report unpaid super contributions from my employer](https://www.ato.gov.au/calculators-and-tools/super-report-unpaid-super-contributions-from-my-employer)

## AU-0128 · Check insurance before combining super accounts

Compare fees, insurance and benefits before consolidating super. Closing an account can end valuable cover; confirm any replacement protection first and check employer benefits and tax consequences. Seek professional advice before transferring a defined benefit account, where leaving may surrender benefits you cannot regain.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Time to compare statements; professional advice may have a fee. |
| Potential value | May simplify super while avoiding an unintended loss of protection. |
| Applies to | People with multiple super accounts or considering a fund change. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Moneysmart: Consolidating super funds](https://moneysmart.gov.au/how-super-works/consolidating-super-funds)

## AU-0129 · Find lost super through the free official route

Check active, lost and unclaimed super through your own myGov account linked to the ATO, or use the official phone and in-person routes. Update retained funds' contact details and check insurance before transferring balances. Finding an account does not remove the rules restricting access to super.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Official searching is free; you do not need to pay a finder. |
| Potential value | May reconnect you with retirement savings and simplify future administration. |
| Applies to | Anyone who has worked and may have an old or unclaimed super account. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Moneysmart: Find lost super](https://moneysmart.gov.au/how-super-works/find-lost-super)

## AU-0130 · Keep tax evidence as you go

Save income records, receipts and notes explaining eligible work expenses throughout the year. Check each deduction's conditions, reimbursement and private-use rules; a deduction reduces taxable income rather than refunding the purchase price. Review pre-filled information when lodging and obtain appropriate help for complex circumstances.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A few minutes regularly; myTax is free and professional fees vary. |
| Potential value | Less reconstruction at tax time and better-supported claims. |
| Applies to | Australian taxpayers; records and deduction rules depend on the expense and year. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Moneysmart: Lodging a tax return](https://moneysmart.gov.au/work-and-tax/lodging-a-tax-return)

## AU-0131 · Understand HELP before making an extra payment

Check your HELP balance and the current year's rules. Payroll withholding is reconciled at tax time, not credited to the loan every payday. Voluntary payments are additional and generally non-refundable; they do not replace compulsory repayments while debt remains. Confirm final balances before updating withholding after full repayment.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Free to review; a voluntary repayment uses money you cannot ordinarily withdraw again. |
| Potential value | Avoids confusing payroll withholding, compulsory repayments and optional repayments. |
| Applies to | People with HELP debt; other study loans may need separate checking. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Study Assist: HELP debt and repayments FAQs](https://www.studyassist.gov.au/faqs)

## AU-0132 · Check ambulance cover before you need it

Check state ambulance arrangements and your actual cover; Medicare does not cover ambulance services. Queensland and Tasmania have state-funded resident arrangements, while other jurisdictions differ. Check interstate travel, non-emergency services, waiting periods and exclusions. In a life-threatening emergency, call 000 and seek help promptly.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Checking is free; the cost of extra cover depends on your circumstances. |
| Potential value | Clarifies a potentially expensive gap and avoids paying twice for equivalent cover. |
| Applies to | Australian residents, particularly after moving interstate or changing insurance. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [PrivateHealth.gov.au: Ambulance cover by state and territory](https://privatehealth.gov.au/health_insurance/what_is_covered/ambulance.htm)

## AU-0133 · Update super beneficiaries after life changes

Check your fund's beneficiary nomination after a separation, new relationship, birth or death. Ask which nominations are permitted, whether yours is binding and when it expires; super does not automatically pass through your will in every case, so coordinate the documents properly.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A fund form; legal advice for complex circumstances. |
| Potential value | Improves the chance your records reflect your current intentions. |
| Applies to | Super members, particularly after family circumstances change. |
| Basis | Practical suggestion |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Moneysmart: Who gets your super if you die](https://moneysmart.gov.au/how-super-works/who-gets-your-super-if-you-die)

## AU-0134 · Read insurance inside super as a real policy

Find the cover amount, premium, occupation category, exclusions and cancellation rules in your fund documents. Ask about any detail that does not match your work or health circumstances, and assess replacement cover before cancelling something you may later struggle to obtain.
