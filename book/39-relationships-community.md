A good life includes connection and enjoyment. Choose activities and arrangements that fit your people, access needs and budget.

## AU-0685 · Make a repeatable invitation

Offer a specific low-pressure plan, such as a local walk or a cuppa, with a clear time. Let people decline without needing an excuse. A simple invitation is easier to repeat than an elaborate event.
