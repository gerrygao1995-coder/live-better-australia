## AU-0325 · Ask who coordinates your chronic-condition care

Ask your regular GP whether a chronic condition management plan would help coordinate goals, reviews and other practitioners. Confirm any eligibility, referral and gap-fee rules before booking allied health; a plan should identify useful care rather than simply become another document to maintain.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Consultation and referred-service fees vary in A$. |
| Potential value | Clarifies priorities and responsibility across providers. |
| Applies to | People with ongoing conditions; current Medicare criteria apply. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Services Australia: GP chronic condition management plan](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/gp-chronic-condition-management-plan?context=20)

## AU-0326 · Have inhaler technique checked in person

Bring your asthma devices to a review and demonstrate how you use them rather than just saying that you know. Ask the clinician or pharmacist to correct technique, check the written plan and discuss symptoms that suggest control is worsening.

## AU-0333 · Learn pacing instead of alternating extremes

Ask a pain professional how to divide activity and rest into a pattern suited to your condition. Note when an ambitious burst leads to a setback, then adjust the plan collaboratively; avoid assuming that everyone should push through worsening symptoms.

## AU-0341 · Build an epilepsy plan for everyday settings

Discuss seizure management, medicine routines and safety with your treating team, then share relevant instructions with agreed supporters. Ask how work, swimming, travel and driving rules apply to you; never stop anti-seizure medicines abruptly or assume a seizure-free period removes all restrictions.

## AU-0342 · Get bowel symptoms assessed before restricting foods

Describe bowel changes, pain and any concerning symptoms to your GP before assuming you have IBS. If a dietary trial is recommended, ask a qualified dietitian about its purpose, duration and reintroduction plan rather than permanently eliminating many foods yourself.
