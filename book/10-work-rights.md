## AU-0163 · Know which pay rules cover your job

Identify your award or agreement, classification and employment type, then compare actual duties and a roster with current rates. Fair Work's tool covers national-system awards. Check coverage first: many state public-sector and council workers, and some WA businesses including sole traders, use state systems; follow the coverage guide to the relevant body.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Free; allow 30 minutes initially. |
| Potential value | Find possible underpayments and assess a job offer on its full conditions. |
| Applies to | Employees; coverage depends on employer and jurisdiction. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Fair Work system](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/about-us/workplace-laws/fair-work-system) · [Pay and Conditions Tool](https://calculate.fairwork.gov.au/)

## AU-0164 · Keep a payslip trail you control

Save contracts, rosters and payslips outside your work account and record actual hours and breaks. Under Commonwealth workplace rules, employers must provide payslips within one working day of payment and keep employee records for seven years. Employees and former employees can request their records; raise discrepancies with dates and figures.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Free; a few minutes each payday. |
| Potential value | Makes payroll corrections and later advice easier. |
| Applies to | Employees covered by the relevant Commonwealth record-keeping rules; state-system obligations require a separate check. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Record-keeping and pay slips](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/tools-and-resources/fact-sheets/rights-and-obligations/record-keeping-pay-slips)

## AU-0165 · Use leave to deal with family violence

Check family and domestic violence leave before using annual leave or giving up work. Under the National Employment Standards, full-time, part-time and casual employees have ten paid days yearly, available immediately and renewed on their employment anniversary without accumulating. Ask about taking leave, notice, evidence and safe handling of records.

| Cost / effort | No application fee; taking leave requires meeting the applicable conditions. |
| Potential value | Time and income to address safety and practical needs. |
| Applies to | Employees entitled under the relevant law; check coverage and any additional state or workplace provisions. |
| Basis | Official rule |

## AU-0166 · Treat a dismissal deadline as urgent

Contact the Fair Work Commission, your union or a legal service immediately after a potentially unfair dismissal: the national application deadline is generally 21 days after dismissal takes effect. Eligibility includes six months' service, or twelve for small-business employees, plus other coverage and award, agreement or earnings conditions. Check other available claims too.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Initial information is free; application and advice fees may apply. |
| Potential value | Preserves the opportunity to seek a remedy. |
| Applies to | Employees; state-system claims and other claim types have their own rules. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

## AU-0167 · Report a workplace hazard to the right place

Record the hazard and raise it through the workplace safety process if safe. Use Safe Work Australia's directory to find your jurisdiction's regulator or compensation authority; Safe Work Australia develops policy rather than enforcing individual cases. After injury, seek care and check the claim process instead of assuming a payroll report completes it.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Official information is free; treatment and claim arrangements vary. |
| Potential value | A clearer route to safer work and injury support. |
| Applies to | Workers; regulator and compensation scheme depend on jurisdiction, employer and industry. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [WHS regulators and workers' compensation authorities](https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/law-and-regulation/whs-regulators-and-workers-compensation-authorities-contact-information)

## AU-0168 · Agree what 'after hours' actually means

Agree normal availability, emergency contact and on-call arrangements in writing. National-system employees, including small-business employees since August 2025, can refuse out-of-hours work contact unless refusal is unreasonable. The reason, disruption, compensation, role and personal circumstances matter; the rule does not prohibit employers sending messages.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Free; a short conversation and written follow-up. |
| Potential value | Protects recovery time while clarifying legitimate availability requirements. |
| Applies to | Employees covered by the national right to disconnect; other arrangements may provide additional rights. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Right to disconnect](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/employment-conditions/right-to-disconnect)

## AU-0169 · Test a job offer against an ordinary week

Sketch the actual week the offer would require: roster, travel, unpaid gaps, caring commitments and recovery time. Ask which hours are guaranteed and which depend on demand, then compare the whole arrangement with your present job. A higher advertised rate may still be a poor personal fit when the timetable is uncertain.

## AU-0170 · Get important recruitment promises in writing

Before resigning elsewhere, ask for the promised salary, employment type, location, start date and any conditions in the written offer. Flag differences between the advertisement, interview discussion and contract, and request clarification before accepting. ## AU-0171 · Check an unpaid trial before attending

Ask what skill you will demonstrate, who will supervise and how long it will take. Fair Work says an unpaid trial must be limited to what is reasonably needed to demonstrate relevant skills under direct supervision; productive work beyond that can require payment. There is no universal free-shift allowance.

## AU-0172 · Question an unexplained wage deduction

Ask payroll to identify the amount, purpose and legal basis before accepting a deduction for breakage, a short till or equipment. Under national rules, deductions need an authorised basis and additional restrictions can apply, even where a document was signed. Keep the payslip and seek Fair Work guidance for your circumstances.

## AU-0173 · Put a flexible-work request into a workable proposal

Describe the change sought, why it is needed and how handovers or customer coverage could work. Certain employees meeting service and circumstance requirements have a statutory right to request flexibility; it is not an automatic right to the exact arrangement. Check Fair Work's process, written-response rules and dispute options before submitting.

## AU-0174 · Check pay-secrecy rules rather than guessing

If someone says you cannot discuss pay, read the applicable contract and Fair Work guidance before responding. Rights to share or decline to share pay information include transitional rules: some unchanged contracts made before 7 December 2022 with secrecy terms are treated differently. Avoid pressuring a colleague to disclose their pay.

## AU-0175 · Write down the outcome of a difficult meeting

After a performance or conduct meeting, record the issues raised, examples provided, actions agreed and the review date. Ask for unclear expectations to be explained with observable examples, and send a calm summary for correction. Keep your own lawful records and obtain advice promptly if dismissal or another serious consequence is raised.

## AU-0176 · Price the training promised by an employer

Ask whether employer-sponsored study includes course fees, materials, travel, time off and any repayment condition if you leave. Compare the written arrangement with how the course fits your work and caring commitments, and get advice on unclear repayment clauses before signing. Confirm who owns or receives the resulting certificate.

## AU-0177 · Build a useful handover before leave

List current tasks, deadlines, contact people and where authorised colleagues can find the necessary files. Agree who can make decisions while you are away and what genuinely needs escalation, without sharing personal passwords. Check the handover with the receiving person so you are not the only one who understands it.

## AU-0178 · Recheck conditions when the job changes

When responsibilities, work location or roster change, compare the new arrangement with your existing written terms. Ask whether the classification, allowances, equipment or travel expectations also need review, and record what is agreed. Seek industrial advice if the change is disputed rather than assuming a new job title settles the matter.

## AU-0179 · Prepare a job-exit checklist

Before your last day, list final-pay questions, leave records, personal files you may lawfully retain, equipment to return and a reference contact. Ask for receipts when returning valuable equipment and confirm where the final payslip will be sent. Keep employer-confidential material out of your personal archive and follow agreed handover requirements.

## AU-0180 · Choose workplace support before the problem grows

Identify a trusted support person, union contact or appropriate legal service before a difficult workplace process escalates. Check membership or service coverage, appointment availability and what documents they need, without assuming representation is automatic. Prepare a short timeline and the outcome you want so an initial conversation is useful.
