## AU-0001 · Call Triple Zero without delaying

Call 000 for a serious, urgent threat to life or safety and request ambulance, police or fire. Give your location first, describe what happened and stay on the line; answer questions even when help feels urgent.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A$0 emergency call; ambulance charges are separate. |
| Potential value | Connects you with the appropriate emergency response. |
| Applies to | Everyone in Australia; a phone needs an available network. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Healthdirect: calling triple zero](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/calling-triple-zero)

## AU-0002 · Make your address easy to communicate

Write your address, unit, entry instructions and a nearby landmark on a household emergency card. Show children and regular visitors where it is, and review the details after moving; on rural trips, know the property or road you are using.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A$0; a short preparation task. |
| Potential value | Reduces confusion about where help is needed. |
| Applies to | Households, workplaces and people travelling in unfamiliar areas. |
| Basis | Practical suggestion |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

## AU-0003 · Learn CPR with practical instruction

Book hands-on CPR training. If someone is unresponsive and not breathing normally, check for danger, call 000 and begin CPR following the operator; occasional gasps are not normal breathing, and breaths are especially important after drowning.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Course fees vary in A$; allow practice time. |
| Potential value | Builds confidence to act before paramedics arrive. |
| Applies to | Bystanders; this reminder is not a complete first-aid lesson. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [ANZCOR: Guideline 8 — Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation](https://www.anzcor.org/home/basic-life-support/guideline-8-cardiopulmonary-resuscitation-cpr) · [Australian Resuscitation Council: CPR and drowning FAQs](https://www.resus.org.au/training-and-education/faq)

## AU-0004 · Locate the nearest AED

Find the automated external defibrillator at work, your club or a frequently visited venue before an emergency occurs. During a suspected cardiac arrest, ask another person to fetch it while CPR continues, then attach it and follow the spoken instructions.

## AU-0005 · Save the poisons advice number

Save 13 11 26 for suspected poisoning or taking the wrong medicine. Keep the container available, do not induce vomiting, and call 000 immediately for unconsciousness, breathing difficulty or serious illness rather than waiting for poisons advice.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Call charges depend on your service. |
| Potential value | Gets advice matched to the actual substance. |
| Applies to | Households and workplaces; prevent exposures with secure storage. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Healthdirect: swallowed substances](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/swallowed-substances)

## AU-0008 · Keep out of floodwater

Choose another route when a road or path is flooded, even if it is familiar or another driver has crossed. Follow emergency warnings and evacuation instructions; if trapped in a life-threatening flood situation, call 000 and describe your location.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A$0 decision; alternative travel may take longer. |
| Potential value | Avoids hidden depth, current and road damage. |
| Applies to | Everyone; NSW SES guidance reflects a wider safety principle. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [NSW SES: During a flood](https://www.ses.nsw.gov.au/during-emergency/during-flood)

## AU-0009 · Plan a cool refuge for extreme heat

Identify a cool place you can reach and arrange check-ins for people needing help. Move strenuous tasks to cooler times; confusion, collapse or seizures in the heat require 000, immediate cooling as directed and no drinks if swallowing is unsafe.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Preparation can cost A$0; cooling and transport vary. |
| Potential value | Reduces exposure and supports a quicker response. |
| Applies to | Especially older people, children, outdoor workers and medically vulnerable people. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Healthdirect: heatstroke](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/heatstroke)

## AU-0010 · Swim where a patrol is operating

Choose a patrolled beach, read the signs and swim between the red and yellow flags. Ask a lifesaver about conditions, supervise children actively and avoid alcohol; if someone is in trouble, alert the patrol or call 000 without attempting an unsafe rescue.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Safety information costs A$0; travel costs vary. |
| Potential value | Adds supervision and local hazard knowledge. |
| Applies to | All beachgoers, including confident local swimmers. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Surf Life Saving NSW: Staying safe at the beach](https://beachsafetyhub.org.au/learn-&-play/learn-play-in-your-language/staying-safe-at-the-beach/)

## AU-0011 · Learn Australian choking first aid

Take a first-aid course that includes infants if you care for one. If someone cannot clear a blockage by coughing or cannot breathe, call 000 and follow the operator's age-appropriate instructions; do not rely on an improvised technique remembered from television.

| Cost / effort | Course fees vary in A$; practical training time. |
| Potential value | Prepares you for a rapidly developing emergency. |
| Applies to | Parents, carers and anyone who prepares or shares food. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Healthdirect: choking](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/choking)

## AU-0012 · Cool burns promptly and seek advice

Remove the heat source only if safe, and cool a thermal burn with cool running water for at least 20 minutes. Do not use ice or home remedies; call 000 for a serious burn and get advice about assessment for smaller burns.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Usually low A$ cost; prompt action matters. |
| Potential value | Limits further heat injury while arranging care. |
| Applies to | Thermal burns; chemical and electrical injuries need specific urgent advice. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Healthdirect: burns and scalds](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/burns-and-scalds)

## AU-0013 · Recognise a possible stroke

Remember sudden facial drooping, arm weakness or speech difficulty as reasons to call 000 immediately. Note when symptoms began or when the person was last well, and do not wait to see whether symptoms disappear before seeking emergency help.

## AU-0014 · Take possible heart-attack symptoms seriously

Call 000 for suspected heart-attack symptoms, including concerning chest discomfort with breathlessness or feeling very unwell. Stop activity and follow the operator; do not drive yourself or wait for a routine appointment because symptoms seem unusual or come and go.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A$0 emergency call; care costs depend on coverage. |
| Potential value | Avoids a dangerous delay in emergency assessment. |
| Applies to | Everyone; symptoms are not always the same between people. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Healthdirect: heart attack](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/heart-attack)

## AU-0016 · Treat a suspected snakebite as an emergency

Call 000 after a suspected snakebite, keep the person still and follow pressure-immobilisation instructions from trained guidance or the operator. Do not wash the bite, cut it, suck it or try to catch the snake; keep yourself safe as well.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A$0 to learn; first-aid kit costs vary. |
| Potential value | Reduces harmful improvisation while help is arranged. |
| Applies to | Anyone outdoors; bites can be difficult to see. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Healthdirect: snake bites](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/snake-bites)

<a id="au-0017"></a>

## AU-0017 · Know what to do around a seizure

Protect the person from nearby hazards, time the seizure and avoid restraining them or putting anything in their mouth. Call 000 for a first seizure, one lasting over five minutes, repeated seizures without recovery, injury or breathing concerns; follow any known care plan.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A$0 preparation; individual plans may need training. |
| Potential value | Supports safer first aid and useful information for clinicians. |
| Applies to | Bystanders and carers; immediate safety comes first. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Healthdirect: seizures](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/seizures)

## AU-0018 · Keep asthma emergency instructions current

Ask your clinician for a written asthma action plan and have your inhaler technique checked. Keep prescribed reliever treatment accessible; severe breathing difficulty or an asthma attack not improving with the plan requires 000, rather than waiting to find a cheaper appointment.
