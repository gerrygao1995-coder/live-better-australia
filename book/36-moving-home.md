# 36. Moving home or city

Treat a move as a sequence of decisions about housing, access, cash and connections. Check local rules for the place you are leaving and the place you are going.

## AU-0638 · Tell Services Australia about a relevant move

If you receive a payment, Services Australia says to report a new address within 14 days. Ask how your new accommodation or household circumstances affect your particular payment rather than assuming it stays the same.
