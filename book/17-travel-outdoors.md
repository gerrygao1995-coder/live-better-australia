## AU-0289 · Compare travel cover against the actual itinerary

Check destinations, activities, medical conditions, exclusions, excesses and cancellation cover. Read the product disclosure statement and ask about anything unclear. The cheapest policy may leave the reason for your trip uncovered.

| Potential value | A better fit between risk and cover. |

## AU-0292 · Leave a useful bushwalking trip plan

Tell a reliable contact your route, group, expected return and what to do if you are overdue. A submitted form alone does not guarantee a search will start. Check the local parks authority’s advice.

## AU-0293 · Prepare for no mobile reception

Plan navigation, communication, water, clothing and first aid for the actual route. Consider a suitable emergency beacon for remote travel, and know how to use it. Downloading a map is only part of preparation.

## AU-0294 · Make road conditions part of the itinerary

Check official warnings and road closures before setting off and during the trip. Allow a safe alternative or a delay. Do not drive, walk or ride into floodwater.
