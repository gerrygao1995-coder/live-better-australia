# 37. Working and studying overseas

For Australians planning a longer stay abroad, check the destination’s rules and the Australian obligations that may continue. This is a planning framework, not immigration advice.

## AU-0649 · Verify work or study permission at the destination

Check the destination government’s rules for the exact activity you plan, including remote work. Ask for clarification before accepting a role or travelling. Do not infer permission from visa-free tourist entry.
