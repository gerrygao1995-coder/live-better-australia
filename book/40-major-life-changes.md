## AU-0703 · Separate urgent decisions from decisions that can wait

Write down what must happen today, what has a verified deadline and what can wait. Ask the relevant professional to explain unfamiliar deadlines. Avoid making every difficult decision in one sitting.

| Potential value | A clearer first day after major news. |
| Applies to | Redundancy, illness, bereavement and other disruption. |

## AU-0704 · Choose one trusted coordination person

If you want help, agree who can organise messages or appointments and what you are comfortable sharing. Keep decisions with the person entitled to make them. Permission to help with logistics is not authority over everything.
