## AU-0181 · Check Free TAFE before paying full fees

Follow government Free TAFE information to your state or territory's current programme. Funding covers selected courses and eligibility groups, not every course or applicant. Confirm your place, tuition assistance, materials, placement costs and conditions in writing before comparing the full cost with other training options.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Eligibility enquiries are free; tuition assistance and extra costs vary. |
| Potential value | May reduce training costs without overlooking practical expenses. |
| Applies to | Prospective VET students; course availability and eligibility differ across jurisdictions and intakes. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Department of Employment and Workplace Relations: Free TAFE](https://www.dewr.gov.au/skills-reform/free-tafe)

## AU-0182 · Check the course behind the advertisement

Check the qualification code and provider on the National Training Register before paying. Ask about assessment, licensing outcomes, support, work placements, refund terms and everything you must supply. Compare the complete timetable and costs with your commitments, and verify any licensing promise with the relevant authority.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Free research; course and licensing costs vary. |
| Potential value | Avoids paying for training that cannot deliver the qualification or pathway you need. |
| Applies to | People choosing vocational education; licensing requirements can differ by state and territory. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [ASQA: choose a course and provider](https://www.asqa.gov.au/for-students/choose-a-course-and-provider)

## AU-0183 · Get credit for skills you already have

Ask about credit transfer for completed study and RPL for skills gained through work, training or life experience. A qualified assessor checks evidence against course requirements; credit is not automatic. Providers must offer an opportunity to apply unless course or licensing rules prevent it, so request requirements, fees and remaining training in writing.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Enquiry is free; assessment charges and evidence-gathering time vary. |
| Potential value | Can avoid unnecessary repeated training while preserving a credible qualification. |
| Applies to | VET students with relevant prior learning; credit is assessed, not automatic. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [ASQA: credit transfer and recognition of prior learning](https://www.asqa.gov.au/for-students/credit-transfer-and-recognition-prior-learning)

## AU-0184 · Recover your training record before retraining

Activate your USI account and download the free, authenticated VET transcript covering nationally recognised training since January 2015. Use it for prerequisites, job applications or credit-transfer enquiries, while retaining original certificates. Ask the provider about missing records before paying to repeat a unit.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Free; allow time to recover account access if necessary. |
| Potential value | Reduces lost-document administration and potentially unnecessary retraining. |
| Applies to | USI account holders with relevant VET records; older training needs separate evidence. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [USI VET transcripts](https://www.usi.gov.au/usi-vet-transcripts)

## AU-0185 · Separate a study subsidy from a study loan

Confirm whether your higher-education offer is Commonwealth supported, the student contribution and your separate HECS-HELP eligibility. Check citizenship, residence and other requirements, and submit the assistance form by the census date. Calendar withdrawal deadlines and compare living costs too; HELP debt is subject to indexation and income-based repayments.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Research is free; contribution amounts and loan eligibility vary. |
| Potential value | Prevents a funding assumption becoming an unexpected bill or debt. |
| Applies to | Higher-education applicants; domestic status and HELP eligibility are not interchangeable. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Study Assist: HECS-HELP](https://www.studyassist.gov.au/financial-and-study-support/hecs-help)

## AU-0186 · Audit apprentice pay as your skills progress

Identify the award or agreement and registered training contract behind an apprentice or trainee rate. Check pay progression against time or competency milestones and ask about training time, equipment and other entitlements. Use national-system Fair Work guidance or the appropriate state authority rather than accepting an unexplained 'trainee rate'.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Free to check; training and equipment arrangements vary. |
| Potential value | Helps you receive the correct pay while earning a qualification. |
| Applies to | Apprentices and trainees; age, training stage, contract and industrial system affect entitlements. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Fair Work: apprentice and trainee pay rates](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/pay-and-wages/minimum-wages/apprentice-and-trainee-pay-rates)

## AU-0188 · Interview someone doing the job

Ask a worker in the occupation what an ordinary week involves, what entry routes employers actually consider and which parts newcomers underestimate. Seek more than one perspective and distinguish their personal experience from formal requirements. Use the conversation to generate questions for providers, rather than treating one person's pathway as a guarantee.

## AU-0189 · Test the timetable before enrolling

Put classes, travel, study, placement and existing commitments into a sample fortnight. Include the weeks with assessments and unavoidable absences, then ask the provider about attendance, catch-up and part-time options. If the plan only works when nothing goes wrong, reconsider the load before paying or taking on debt.

## AU-0190 · Ask who finds the placement

Get written details of any required placement: hours, location range, supervision, checks, equipment and who secures it. Ask what happens if a suitable placement is delayed or unavailable, and how that affects completion and fees. ## AU-0194 · Choose one useful skill experiment

## AU-0196 · Negotiate a study plan at work

Before reducing hours or accepting extra shifts, show your manager the assessment and placement calendar and propose a workable schedule. Confirm whether the arrangement is temporary, how changes will be handled and when it will be reviewed. Keep course attendance obligations and work commitments visible together rather than negotiating each clash at the last moment.

## AU-0197 · Plan a course pause before withdrawing

If the course becomes unmanageable, contact the provider about deferral, reduced load, withdrawal dates and support options. Ask for the financial and academic consequences in writing and keep proof of any request you submit. Compare your actual options before disappearing from classes, because a missed attendance record is not the same as an approved change.

## AU-0198 · Turn a rejection into a specific next step

After an unsuccessful application, request feedback if offered and compare your evidence with the selection criteria. Choose one change you can control, such as a clearer example, relevant project or better targeted role, rather than buying another course immediately. Track a few applications to see patterns before deciding the entire pathway is wrong.
