## AU-0613 · Remove private details before using a public AI tool

Use invented names and placeholder data when the task does not need real personal information. Follow workplace policy for customer or staff records. For covered organisations, privacy obligations also apply when AI is involved.

| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A brief preparation step. |
| Potential value | Less unnecessary exposure of personal information. |
| Applies to | Personal tasks and workplace use. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

## AU-0618 · Verify a surprising voice or video request independently

If a supposed relative or colleague urgently requests money or access, contact them by a trusted separate route. Synthetic audio and imagery mean apparent familiarity alone is insufficient.
