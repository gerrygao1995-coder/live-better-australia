## AU-0073 · Make unit prices your default comparison

Compare grocery prices using the same unit: per kilogram, litre or item. Most large supermarkets and online grocery stores must display unit prices, although exemptions apply. Choose a larger pack only when you can afford, store and use it, and note normal prices for a few staples.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A few extra minutes while shopping; no subscription needed. |
| Potential value | Makes pack sizes and promotions easier to compare. |
| Applies to | Grocery shoppers across Australia; availability differs at smaller retailers. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [ACCC: Unit prices for groceries](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/pricing/unit-prices-for-groceries)

## AU-0074 · Plan around what is already in the kitchen

Check the fridge, freezer and pantry before shopping, then choose meals sharing ingredients and write a list. Include a low-effort option and compare suitable fresh, frozen or tinned ingredients. Review what was discarded before buying larger quantities; include delivery fees where delivery makes shopping workable for you.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | About ten minutes of planning and whatever food your budget allows. |
| Potential value | Can reduce duplicate purchases, waste and last-minute spending. |
| Applies to | Households with different cooking capacity, dietary needs and storage space. |
| Basis | Practical suggestion |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Moneysmart: Ways to save on food and fuel](https://moneysmart.gov.au/budgeting/ways-to-save-on-food-and-fuel)

## AU-0075 · Compare the whole energy bill

Compare whole-bill costs using actual usage, tariffs and supply charges. Energy Made Easy covers NSW, Queensland, SA, Tasmania and the ACT; retail choice varies locally. Victoria uses Victorian Energy Compare. For WA and the NT, follow the national guide, and check solar, demand charges and contract conditions.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A short bill review; official comparison services are free. |
| Potential value | May reduce a recurring cost without reducing essential heating or cooling. |
| Applies to | Energy customers; embedded networks and some regional areas have different options. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Energy Made Easy: Frequently asked questions](https://www.energymadeeasy.gov.au/frequently-asked-questions) · [Australian Government: Switch to save](https://www.energy.gov.au/households/find-the-best-energy-deal) · [Victorian Energy Compare](https://compare.energy.vic.gov.au/)

<a id="au-0076"></a>

## AU-0076 · Buy the phone plan you actually use

Compare recent data use and required coverage with each plan's Critical Information Summary. Check full costs, contract length, cancellation conditions and any separate handset repayments. Save the terms and set a reminder before promotional pricing ends so the next bill remains a deliberate choice.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | One review session; switching costs depend on your contract. |
| Potential value | Can remove payment for allowances or features you rarely use. |
| Applies to | Mobile and home internet customers, especially after a promotion or contract ends. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [ACMA: Understand your phone or internet contract](https://www.acma.gov.au/understand-your-telco-contract)

<a id="au-0077"></a>

## AU-0077 · Check the library before buying another subscription

Ask your council library about borrowing, digital resources, study space and programmes before buying another subscription. NSW legislation provides for free public-library membership, loans and information access, with specified exceptions. Check local eligibility and charges elsewhere, especially for printing or other extra services.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Core services may be free; printing, bookings and other extras can cost money. |
| Potential value | More reading, learning and shared space within a small budget. |
| Applies to | Local residents; the legal example here is specifically NSW. |
| Basis | Practical suggestion |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [State Library of NSW: Library Act and Regulation](https://pls.sl.nsw.gov.au/about/library-act-and-regulation)

## AU-0078 · Use your consumer rights when something fails

Keep proof of purchase and tell the selling business what failed and the remedy you seek. Consumer guarantees apply alongside warranties, but the remedy depends on the problem, including whether it is major or minor. Use ACCC guidance for escalation; change-of-mind returns are different.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Time to document the problem and contact the business. |
| Potential value | May avoid paying to replace or repair a purchase unnecessarily. |
| Applies to | Covered consumer purchases; private sales and other exceptions need separate checking. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [ACCC: Problem with a product or service you bought](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/problem-with-a-product-or-service-you-bought)

## AU-0079 · Give annual bills their own calendar

List renewal dates for insurance, rego, memberships and annual services. Add an earlier reminder to compare options before a payment is taken, and record whether cancellation needs notice rather than assuming turning off a payment cancels the contract.

| Potential value | Fewer forgotten renewals and last-minute decisions under pressure. |
| Applies to | Households with subscriptions, memberships or annual bills. |
| Basis | Practical suggestion |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

## AU-0080 · Set aside predictable irregular costs

Add up known expenses that do not arrive every month, then divide them across your pay cycles. Use a labelled account or a written running balance, and adjust the amount after each real bill rather than treating last year's estimate as fixed.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A calculation and an affordable regular allocation. |
| Potential value | Shows which future expenses are already funded and which need planning. |
| Applies to | People managing rego, school costs, rates or annual premiums. |
| Basis | Practical suggestion |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Moneysmart: How to do a budget](https://moneysmart.gov.au/budgeting/how-to-do-a-budget)

## AU-0082 · Separate the bill date from the pay date

Put upcoming bills beside expected pay dates and identify any gap before money is due. Ask the provider about another payment date or bill smoothing, then compare the full annual cost and conditions before accepting the arrangement.

## AU-0083 · Use a waiting list for optional purchases

## AU-0084 · Price convenience honestly

For a service you use often, record the total charge including delivery, booking, processing and tips. Compare it with a realistic alternative that accounts for your available time, mobility and care responsibilities, then choose deliberately rather than labelling all convenience wasteful.

| Applies to | Anyone balancing household costs with limited time or capacity. |

| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

## AU-0089 · Make a repair decision before shopping

Ask for a repair quote and compare it with replacement, delivery, installation and disposal costs. Include the age, reliability and suitability of the item, and check consumer rights first when a recent purchase has failed; the cheapest sticker price is only one input.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A quote; diagnostic charges may apply. |
| Potential value | Gives repair a fair comparison with the complete replacement cost. |
| Applies to | Owners of faulty household goods that can be safely assessed. |
| Basis | Practical suggestion |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [ACCC: Consumer rights and guarantees](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/buying-products-and-services/consumer-rights-and-guarantees)

## AU-0090 · Check one statement line you cannot explain

Choose an unfamiliar fee or recurring payment and ask the provider what it covers. Verify the answer against the account terms, then remove an unwanted service through the proper process; if a transaction looks unauthorised, contact your bank promptly.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A short review and possibly a phone call. |
| Potential value | Can uncover forgotten services, avoidable charges or errors. |
| Applies to | People with access to bank or card statements. |
| Basis | Practical suggestion |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Moneysmart: Transaction accounts and debit cards](https://moneysmart.gov.au/banking/transaction-accounts-and-debit-cards)
