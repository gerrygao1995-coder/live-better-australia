# 9. Help when money is tight and disability support

Support is part of the system, and asking early can help. Eligibility depends on the programme and your circumstances. Keep applications and evidence organised, and ask for accessible help when needed.

## AU-0145 · Recheck support after a life change

Use Services Australia's Payment and Service Finder after changes in work, income, care, study or health. Read each relevant payment's conditions and gather required evidence, asking for accessible help if needed. Retain claim references; a tool result is a starting point, not an approval.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Free to check; applications take time and may need supporting records. |
| Potential value | Can identify support worth investigating after a change in circumstances. |
| Applies to | People checking potential government support; residence, income and other tests may apply. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Services Australia: Payment and Service Finder](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/paymentfinder) · [Moneysmart: Managing on a low income](https://moneysmart.gov.au/budgeting/managing-on-a-low-income)

## AU-0146 · Keep Rent Assistance details current

Keep Services Australia's rent, address and accommodation details current if you receive an eligible payment. Rent Assistance depends on payment and accommodation conditions and is not available to every renter. Supply a tenancy agreement or Rent Certificate if requested, and report later changes.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Free to update; time to gather rental evidence if required. |
| Potential value | Helps eligible people receive the right amount and reduces avoidable correction problems. |
| Applies to | Recipients or applicants for eligible payments who pay eligible accommodation costs. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Services Australia: Rent Assistance](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/rent-assistance)

## AU-0147 · Look for concessions where you actually live

Use Moneysmart's links to your state or territory concessions directory. Check each programme's conditions and application requirements, then verify approved concessions appear on relevant bills. Keep confirmation and recheck after moving or changing circumstances; programmes within the same state can use different eligibility rules.

| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Free to check; some concessions require an application or renewal. |
| Potential value | May reduce recurring essential costs for eligible households. |
| Applies to | Potential concession holders and people on lower incomes; eligibility is programme-specific. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Moneysmart: State and territory concessions and assistance](https://moneysmart.gov.au/budgeting/managing-on-a-low-income)

## AU-0148 · Use free financial counselling before paying for debt help

Contact the National Debt Helpline on 1800 007 007 for free, confidential financial counselling. Bring available income, expense and debt information plus urgent notices. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples can also use Mob Strong Debt Help on 1800 808 488; check current service availability.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | The counselling service is free; check the official page for current availability. |
| Potential value | Practical support with debt decisions and conversations that can feel overwhelming alone. |
| Applies to | People experiencing financial difficulty; urgent legal proceedings also need legal advice. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Moneysmart: Financial counselling](https://moneysmart.gov.au/managing-debt/financial-counselling)

## AU-0149 · Seek help for today's essentials

Use official emergency-relief and state housing links when essentials or accommodation are unaffordable. Explain what is urgent and ask about evidence, appointments and alternative referrals if help is unavailable. A Centrelink advance brings forward existing payments and reduces later payments; it is not extra income.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Relief services may be free; availability and eligibility depend on the provider. |
| Potential value | Can help meet immediate needs while a longer-term plan is arranged. |
| Applies to | People in financial crisis; an application is not a guarantee of accommodation or funds. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Moneysmart: Urgent help with money](https://moneysmart.gov.au/managing-debt/urgent-help-with-money)

## AU-0150 · Check NDIS and DSP as separate pathways

Check NDIS supports and Disability Support Pension separately: each has its own eligibility and evidence requirements. Eligibility for one does not establish the other. Follow the relevant agency's guidance, retain decisions and ask about reviews. An NDIS partner may also identify community and mainstream support.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Checking is free; gathering clinical evidence can involve time or appointment costs. |
| Potential value | Helps direct effort towards the right application and possible alternatives. |
| Applies to | People with disability and their supporters; diagnosis alone does not establish entitlement. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [NDIS: Eligibility requirements](https://ndis.gov.au/applying/eligibility-requirements/what-are-ndis-eligibility-requirements) · [Services Australia: Disability Support Pension](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/disability-support-pension)

## AU-0151 · Ask about No Interest Loans for an essential item

Before using high-cost credit for an essential purchase, check the No Interest Loans pathway and its current eligibility rules. Ask what purchases it covers, how the supplier is paid and whether repayments fit your budget; it is a loan, not a cash grant.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Eligibility assessment and affordable repayments; no interest or fees under the scheme. |
| Potential value | May offer a lower-cost route to an essential good or service. |
| Applies to | Eligible people needing covered essentials; approval is not automatic. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Moneysmart: No Interest Loans](https://moneysmart.gov.au/loans/no-interest-loans)

## AU-0152 · Prepare a one-page help summary

Write the urgent issue, next deadline, income, essential expenses and the outcome you need in one page. Bring available notices to your appointment and say what is missing, so the helper can identify immediate priorities without waiting for perfect records.

| Applies to | People seeking financial counselling or debt assistance. |
| Basis | Practical suggestion |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

## AU-0153 · Ask a utility provider for its hardship pathway

Explain that payment is difficult and ask for the hardship team, payment options and relevant concessions. Request written details and only agree to an amount you can afford; keep the account number and contact record for any follow-up or complaint.

| At a glance | Details |

| Potential value | May help preserve essential services while payment options are considered. |
| Applies to | People struggling with electricity, gas, water or telecommunications bills. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Moneysmart: Problems paying bills and fines](https://moneysmart.gov.au/managing-debt/problems-paying-your-bills-and-fines)

## AU-0156 · Get support for financial abuse safely

If someone controls your money or coerces financial decisions, contact specialist support using a safe channel. Ask your bank about its support team and use the financial-abuse referrals; plan changes with help if account activity or messages could be monitored.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Specialist support may be free; choose a safe contact method. |
| Potential value | Connects financial problems with appropriate safety and legal support. |
| Applies to | People experiencing coercion, financial control or misuse of money. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Moneysmart: Financial abuse](https://moneysmart.gov.au/family-and-relationships/financial-abuse)

## AU-0157 · Check a debt-help firm's fees before signing

Ask what the business will actually do, what you must pay and whether free financial counselling can provide the help you need. Read cancellation terms and any new loan documents separately, especially when the service proposes refinancing as the solution.

<a id="au-0161"></a>

## AU-0161 · Check the repayment effect of an advance

Before accepting an advance, ask for the schedule showing how later payments change. Compare those future amounts with essential expenses and other deductions, so solving this week's shortfall does not leave the next several payments unmanageable.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A calculation; an advance must be repaid through later payments. |
| Potential value | Makes the future cash-flow effect visible before accepting money early. |
| Applies to | Eligible payment recipients considering an advance. |
| Basis | Practical suggestion |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Moneysmart: Urgent help with money](https://moneysmart.gov.au/managing-debt/urgent-help-with-money)

## AU-0162 · Bring a trusted supporter with clear boundaries

If you want someone to help with a call or appointment, ask the agency how to authorise that support for the task. Decide what they may see or do, retain your own copies and avoid sharing personal passwords as a substitute for the proper arrangement.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A consent discussion and agency-specific set-up. |
| Potential value | Lets you receive help while keeping your role and preferences clear. |
| Applies to | People choosing assistance with money or disability administration. |
| Basis | Practical suggestion |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Services Australia — someone to act for you with Centrelink or aged care](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/someone-to-act-for-you-with-centrelink-or-aged-care?context=22526)
