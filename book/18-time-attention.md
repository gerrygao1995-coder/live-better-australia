## AU-0307 · Give recurring admin one home

Use one calendar or list for renewals, appointments and deadlines. Keep the related documents in a predictable place. Choose a system you will actually open rather than a complex one that needs its own administration.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Setup and brief regular review. |
| Potential value | Less time hunting for what comes next. |
| Applies to | Busy households and individuals. |
| Basis | Practical suggestion |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |
