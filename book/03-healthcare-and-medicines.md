## AU-0037 · Confirm the appointment gap before booking

Ask whether your particular service will be bulk billed and whether that applies to you. Otherwise request the total fee, expected rebate and amount payable on the day; mention any concession card and keep the invoice for checking your claim.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A$0 to ask; appointment costs vary. |
| Potential value | Reduces surprise out-of-pocket charges. |
| Applies to | Medicare patients; not every service or provider offers bulk billing. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Services Australia: Bulk billing](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/bulk-billing?context=60092)

## AU-0038 · Register eligible family members for Medicare Safety Nets

Check whether you should register as a couple or family so eligible medical costs can be combined. Sharing a Medicare card does not register a Safety Net family; keep details current and respond to requests to confirm family members.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A$0 registration; occasional administration. |
| Potential value | May bring higher eligible benefits forward. |
| Applies to | Medicare families and couples; definitions and thresholds apply. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Services Australia: Register for Medicare Safety Nets](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/how-to-register-for-medicare-safety-nets?context=22001)

## AU-0039 · Track the separate PBS Safety Net

Ask your pharmacist how to record eligible medicine spending and combine eligible family costs for the PBS Safety Net. Keep records across pharmacies and check the current application process; it is a separate scheme from Medicare Safety Nets for medical services.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A$0 administration; medicine costs vary. |
| Potential value | Helps access cheaper eligible medicines after the threshold. |
| Applies to | PBS users; annual thresholds and eligible-item rules apply. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Services Australia: When you spend a lot on PBS medicines](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/when-you-spend-lot-pbs-medicines?context=22016)

<a id="au-0040"></a>

## AU-0040 · Ask whether a generic medicine suits you

Ask the pharmacist whether a lower-cost interchangeable generic is appropriate for your prescription. Mention previous reactions or allergies because inactive ingredients and appearance can differ; confirm the medicine's identity so a packaging change does not lead to accidental duplication.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Actual A$ prices and potential savings vary. |
| Potential value | May reduce costs while maintaining intended treatment. |
| Applies to | Prescription users; pharmacist and prescriber restrictions still apply. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [TGA: Generic prescription medicines](https://www.tga.gov.au/news/news-articles/generic-prescription-medicines-fact-sheet)

<a id="au-0041"></a>

## AU-0041 · Discuss a 60-day prescription for stable treatment

Ask your prescriber whether an eligible medicine and stable ongoing condition make a 60-day prescription suitable. A new prescription and clinical assessment are required; a larger supply changes collection frequency, not the dose you should take.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A$ savings vary by medicine and funding circumstances. |
| Potential value | May reduce dispensing expense and pharmacy trips. |
| Applies to | Eligible medicines and suitable patients; not a universal entitlement. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Department of Health: Eligibility for 60-day prescriptions](https://www.health.gov.au/cheaper-medicines/60-day-prescriptions)

| Potential value | Can simplify understanding and reduce medicine errors. |
| Applies to | People using multiple medicines or struggling with their routine. |

| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

<a id="au-0043"></a>

## AU-0043 · Return unwanted medicines to a pharmacy

Check expiry dates and separate medicines you no longer need for return to a community pharmacy. Do not put them in household rubbish or down the drain; arrange replacement essential supplies before discarding them and ask about special disposal for sharps.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Medicine returns are generally A$0; sharps arrangements vary. |
| Potential value | Reduces unsafe storage and environmental contamination. |
| Applies to | All households with medicines, including over-the-counter products. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Healthdirect: out of date medicines](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/out-of-date-medicines)

## AU-0044 · Check ambulance cover when circumstances change

Review state-funded coverage, concessions, subscriptions or insurance before moving or travelling interstate. Ask about treatment without transport, air ambulance and exclusions; Medicare does not cover ambulance, but an emergency still requires calling 000 immediately rather than investigating costs.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A$0 review; premiums and bills vary. |
| Potential value | Finds gaps or duplicate cover before a trip. |
| Applies to | Australian residents; coverage depends on location and circumstances. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [PrivateHealth.gov.au: Ambulance cover](https://privatehealth.gov.au/health_insurance/what_is_covered/ambulance.htm)

## AU-0047 · Find urgent care before the need arises

Locate your nearest Medicare Urgent Care Clinic and check its hours. These clinics handle urgent problems that are not life-threatening when timely GP care is unavailable; for a life-threatening illness or injury, call 000 or attend an emergency department instead.

| Potential value | Adds a practical local care option. |
| Applies to | Check local services and Medicare arrangements before relying on access. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Department of Health: Find a Medicare Urgent Care Clinic](https://www.health.gov.au/find-a-medicare-ucc)

## AU-0052 · Seek another opinion before a major decision

If a proposed non-urgent treatment leaves you uncertain, ask about obtaining another qualified opinion and what information should accompany it. Compare the reasons, likely outcomes and alternatives, including waiting; do not postpone emergency treatment while pursuing extra opinions.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Consultation and record-transfer costs may apply. |
| Potential value | Can clarify options for a consequential choice. |
| Applies to | Patients considering significant treatment where time permits. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Healthdirect: your healthcare rights](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/your-healthcare-rights)

## AU-0053 · Ask for an explanation you can use

Before consenting, ask the clinician to explain the purpose, material risks, alternatives and expected costs in plain language. Tell them what matters to you and request communication support when needed; understanding the decision matters more than silently agreeing to unfamiliar terms.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | A$0 to request information; interpreter arrangements vary. |
| Potential value | Supports informed decisions and partnership. |
| Applies to | Everyone receiving healthcare in Australia. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [Australian Commission on Safety and Quality in Health Care: Understanding your healthcare rights](https://www.safetyandquality.gov.au/your-rights/understanding-your-healthcare-rights)

| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |
