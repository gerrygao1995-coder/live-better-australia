## AU-0271 · Speak to potential customers before buying equipment

Describe the problem you solve and ask prospective customers how they deal with it now. Test a small offer you can deliver before taking on substantial stock or premises.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | Research time and a modest test budget. |
| Potential value | Earlier feedback on real demand. |
| Applies to | New business ideas. |
| Basis | Official / professional guidance |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |

**Read the source:** [business.gov.au — Do market research](https://business.gov.au/marketing-and-advertising/do-market-research)

## AU-0274 · Apply for an ABN through the official route

Check whether you are entitled to an ABN and apply through the Australian Business Register if needed. Government ABN registration is free; optional third-party assistance is a separate service.

| At a glance | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Cost / effort | No government ABN application fee. |
| Potential value | Avoidable intermediary charges can be avoided. |
| Applies to | Eligible businesses and enterprises. |
| Basis | Official rule |
| Editorial review | 2026-10-09 |
