# Find your situation

You do not need to read 720 actions. Pick the situation nearest to yours, then choose one next step. These are editorial reading routes, not individual case plans. If there is immediate danger, call **000** and use [the emergency chapter](book/01-emergencies.md).

## 1. Bills are piling up

Start with essential payments and a clear picture of what is due. Ask about hardship help before an arrears problem grows.

1. [Everyday costs](book/05-everyday-costs.md) — Start with the purchases and bills that repeat. A useful saving fits your household, preserves what you need and takes less effort than it gives back.
2. [Banking and debt](book/07-banking-and-debt.md) — Aim for a system you can understand and maintain. Start with cash needed for essentials, understand the commitments already in place, and ask for help early when repayments no longer fit.
3. [Help when money is tight and disability support](book/09-money-help-and-disability-support.md) — Support is part of the system, and asking early can help. Eligibility depends on the programme and your circumstances. Keep applications and evidence organised, and ask for accessible help when needed.

## 2. I have just lost my job

Keep the relevant records, check time-sensitive rights, and make a short plan for the next pay cycle.

1. [Work rights](book/10-work-rights.md) — A useful pay rise can start with receiving what you are already owed. Identify your employment system, keep your own records and act promptly when something goes wrong.
2. [Help when money is tight and disability support](book/09-money-help-and-disability-support.md) — Support is part of the system, and asking early can help. Eligibility depends on the programme and your circumstances. Keep applications and evidence organised, and ask for accessible help when needed.
3. [Education and career](book/11-education-and-career.md) — Choose training by the work it enables, the support available and its full cost. A subsidised place or recognition of existing skills can change the calculation considerably.

## 3. I am renting, buying or moving

Check the paperwork, total costs and the rules for the state or territory where the home is located.

1. [Renting and buying a home](book/06-renting-and-buying-a-home.md) — Housing choices affect cash flow, time and security. Compare the full cost and keep good records. Rental and property law depends on the state or territory and the type of arrangement.
2. [Moving home or city](book/36-moving-home.md) — Treat a move as a sequence of decisions about housing, access, cash and connections. Check local rules for the place you are leaving and the place you are going.
3. [Climate, disasters and resilient homes](book/26-climate-disasters-and-resilient-homes.md) — Prepare for the hazards that affect your address and the people who live there. Start with reliable warnings and an achievable plan, then evaluate building improvements and insurance with the right local expertise.

## 4. I need healthcare without a maze

Prepare the appointment, check likely charges and use the right service for the problem.

1. [Healthcare and medicines](book/03-healthcare-and-medicines.md) — Understand the service, the bill and the next step. Medicare, PBS and state programmes have different eligibility and cost rules.
2. [Chronic conditions](book/19-chronic-conditions.md) — Use personalised plans, coordinated care and reviews that focus on everyday function. This chapter does not diagnose conditions or recommend medicine doses.
3. [Mental health and energy](book/04-mental-health-and-energy.md) — Professional care, rest, safer work and connection can all matter. Seek support that fits your circumstances, and use emergency services for immediate danger.

## 5. We are having a baby

Start with care, leave arrangements and the first weeks at home; buy around real needs.

1. [Pregnancy and birth](book/20-pregnancy-and-birth.md) — Seek early, individual maternity advice and compare local care options. Decisions, medical needs and available services differ; support should respect the pregnant person's preferences.
2. [Babies and early childhood](book/21-babies-and-early-childhood.md) — Spend attention on safe sleep, feeding support, development and preventable hazards. Ask your child-health team about individual needs rather than buying every advertised solution.
3. [Relationships and children](book/12-relationships-and-children.md) — Good household decisions make money, care and responsibilities discussable. Plan around the people involved, and use specialist help when safety or legal rights are at stake.

## 6. My child is getting older

Update the plan for health, school, independence and online life as your child develops.

1. [School age and teenagers](book/22-school-age-and-teenagers.md) — Build health skills gradually, listen to young people and coordinate support with schools and clinicians. Privacy, consent and services vary with age and circumstances.
2. [Relationships and children](book/12-relationships-and-children.md) — Good household decisions make money, care and responsibilities discussable. Plan around the people involved, and use specialist help when safety or legal rights are at stake.
3. [AI, privacy and digital judgement](book/35-ai-privacy.md) — Use digital tools where you can inspect the result. Keep personal judgement, consent and accountability with people.

## 7. A relationship has become unsafe or ended

Start with immediate safety and confidential support, then records and suitable legal help.

1. [Emergencies](book/01-emergencies.md) — Prepare before a crisis. In immediate danger, call Triple Zero (000) and follow the operator; these brief entries do not replace practical first-aid training.
2. [Separation and family law](book/30-separation-and-family-law.md) — Separation involves several decisions with different purposes and deadlines. Organise safety, care, daily money and formal advice without relying on a friend's settlement as a rule for yours.
3. [Consumer and legal rights](book/14-consumer-and-legal-rights.md) — A low price is poor value if you cannot use the product or resolve a problem. Keep evidence, know which rights apply and send complaints to the body that can actually help.

## 8. A parent needs more help

Ask what they want, check care pathways, and agree on how responsibilities will be shared.

1. [Ageing and bereavement](book/13-ageing-and-bereavement.md) — Small preparations can spare a family difficult decisions during illness or grief. Start with assessed needs, documented wishes and clear responsibilities rather than the first product or service offered.
2. [Disability health and access](book/23-disability-health-and-access.md) — Health care should work with the person’s communication, access needs and choices. NDIS funding and health-system responsibilities are distinct, and eligibility must be checked for the actual support.
3. [Retirement transitions](book/28-retirement-transitions.md) — Retirement involves time, housing, relationships and administration as well as money. Use your own needs and the current rules to plan; there is no single balance or retirement age that fits everyone.

## 9. I may have been scammed

Contact the relevant provider through an independent official channel and act on any urgent account risk.

1. [Secure accounts and devices](book/15-digital-security.md) — Protect the accounts that unlock your money, identity and memories. Start with your main email account.
2. [Banking and debt](book/07-banking-and-debt.md) — Aim for a system you can understand and maintain. Start with cash needed for essentials, understand the commitments already in place, and ask for help early when repayments no longer fit.
3. [Consumer and legal rights](book/14-consumer-and-legal-rights.md) — A low price is poor value if you cannot use the product or resolve a problem. Keep evidence, know which rights apply and send complaints to the body that can actually help.

## 10. I am turning 18

Set up essential records, compare your next steps and make a realistic budget for independence.

1. [Turning 18 and choosing a path](book/33-turning-18-and-choosing-a-path.md) — You do not need your whole life settled at eighteen. Get control of essential records, understand which rules actually apply and choose a next step you can review.
2. [Education and career](book/11-education-and-career.md) — Choose training by the work it enables, the support available and its full cost. A subsidised place or recognition of existing skills can change the calculation considerably.
3. [Banking and debt](book/07-banking-and-debt.md) — Aim for a system you can understand and maintain. Start with cash needed for essentials, understand the commitments already in place, and ask for help early when repayments no longer fit.

## 11. I want to start a side business

Test demand, count all costs and understand the work, tax and customer obligations.

1. [Starting a small business](book/16-small-business.md) — Check the demand, obligations and cash before committing to overheads. A side project still needs a clear business model.
2. [Gig work, contracting and self-employment rights](book/32-gig-work-contracting-and-self-employment-rights.md) — A contractor's hourly quote is only part of the arrangement. Establish your status, responsibilities, real costs and dispute options before deciding whether the work is worthwhile.
3. [Websites, apps and online projects](book/38-online-projects.md) — Build something maintainable, useful and clear about its responsibilities. These checks also help volunteer groups running a small online service.

## 12. I am moving overseas

Check entry and work permissions, healthcare, financial obligations and a practical return plan.

1. [Working and studying overseas](book/37-overseas-work-study.md) — For Australians planning a longer stay abroad, check the destination’s rules and the Australian obligations that may continue. This is a planning framework, not immigration advice.
2. [Travel and outdoor safety](book/17-travel-outdoors.md) — A good trip includes a workable way home. Match the plan to the people, conditions and location.
3. [Super, tax and insurance](book/08-super-tax-and-insurance.md) — A few administrative checks can prevent costly oversights. Rules, thresholds and policy terms change, so use the linked official guidance and your own documents before moving money or changing cover.

## 13. Retirement is getting closer

Work through spending, income, care, housing and the use of your time together.

1. [Retirement transitions](book/28-retirement-transitions.md) — Retirement involves time, housing, relationships and administration as well as money. Use your own needs and the current rules to plan; there is no single balance or retirement age that fits everyone.
2. [Super, tax and insurance](book/08-super-tax-and-insurance.md) — A few administrative checks can prevent costly oversights. Rules, thresholds and policy terms change, so use the linked official guidance and your own documents before moving money or changing cover.

## 14. I live outside a capital city

Factor in distance, service availability, transport, climate and the real cost of access.

1. [Regional and remote living](book/29-regional-and-remote-living.md) — Distance changes how services, travel and household logistics work. Build plans around your actual community and available services, and check access before committing money or a long journey.
2. [Transport and car ownership](book/25-transport-and-car-ownership.md) — Getting around well means matching transport to your location, budget and abilities. Compare real journeys and full ownership costs, while keeping maintenance and road safety central.
3. [Climate, disasters and resilient homes](book/26-climate-disasters-and-resilient-homes.md) — Prepare for the hazards that affect your address and the people who live there. Start with reliable warnings and an achievable plan, then evaluate building improvements and insurance with the right local expertise.

## 15. Life has taken a major turn

Reduce the immediate load, find the next useful contact and work in manageable steps.

1. [Getting through major life changes](book/40-major-life-changes.md) — Use these planning prompts alongside the relevant health, work, legal and support chapters. Urgent danger needs emergency help, not a checklist.
2. [Mental health and energy](book/04-mental-health-and-energy.md) — Professional care, rest, safer work and connection can all matter. Seek support that fits your circumstances, and use emergency services for immediate danger.
3. [Time, attention and life admin](book/18-time-attention.md) — These are optional practical routines. Try the ones that remove friction in your circumstances; the suggested benefits are editorial judgements.

## 16. I want everyday life to feel easier

Fix one recurring frustration, keep what works and make space for people and recreation.

1. [Time, attention and life admin](book/18-time-attention.md) — These are optional practical routines. Try the ones that remove friction in your circumstances; the suggested benefits are editorial judgements.
2. [Food and household value](book/27-food-and-household-value.md) — Use what you own well, keep food safe and compare the useful life of household purchases. Lower cost is helpful only when the item remains safe, suitable and manageable for your household.
3. [Relationships, community and recreation](book/39-relationships-community.md) — A good life includes connection and enjoyment. Choose activities and arrangements that fit your people, access needs and budget.
