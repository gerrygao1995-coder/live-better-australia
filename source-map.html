This is a thematic adaptation: every original chapter has a destination, but individual recommendations have been selected, rewritten, split, combined or omitted. A mapped theme does not claim that every original detail is reproduced. Australian institutions, state differences and applicable official sources replace mainland-China-specific rules.

Deliberate changes

  • Australian healthcare pathways and current program guidance replace Chinese screening schedules, insurance pathways and emergency contacts. Blanket treatment and dosing instructions are not carried over.
  • Federal and state or territory distinctions replace assumptions of a single national tenancy, transport, legal or eligibility rule.
  • Work rights, super, HELP, Australian business obligations and local support services take the place of the original country-specific systems.
  • Transport ownership, disaster preparation, regional access, retirement transitions, AI judgement and moving home receive explicit coverage.
  • Every action has applicability, effort, potential value and a basis label. The project also adds situation-based routes and a searchable offline reader.
