Useful, with the context attached

What do the evidence labels mean?

Official rule points to a requirement or process. Official / professional guidance points to advice from a relevant primary source. Practical suggestion is editorial judgement. These are different kinds of support, not quality scores.

Is this a government website?

No. This is an independent, community-editable guide. Official sources are linked so you can check them directly. The project has no government or upstream endorsement.

Can I share it?

Yes. Share the page that fits someone's situation. Text is available under CC BY 4.0 with attribution. Please retain sources, limits and credits when adapting it.
